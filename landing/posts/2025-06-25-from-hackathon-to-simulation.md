In late 2018, I had the opportunity to participate in a national hackathon hosted by the New Zealand Transport Agency (NZTA), themed **"Save One More Life."** The objective was simple but powerful: create digital strategies to improve road safety outcomes for drivers, pedestrians, and cyclists. But what began as a high-pressure, design-led sprint evolved into a long-form journey into autonomous vehicle simulation and cloud-integrated safety systems.

The post covers the hackathon concept, the follow-up lane detection pipeline built with Python, OpenCV and the CARLA simulator, and how cloud architecture principles shaped the way its data was secured and packaged.
